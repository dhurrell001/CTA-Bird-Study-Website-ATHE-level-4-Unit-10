Why are we studying birds?

The CEA and CTO are working together to collect bird sighting data from residents across Centrala to assist in urban planning, and to create positive change and encourage more wildlife (including birds) into Centrala.

Living spaces with access to nature, and in particular birds, can have many positive effects on the residents mental health and physical wellbeing.

This information will help build a clearer picture of bird activity in urban and suburban areas.
